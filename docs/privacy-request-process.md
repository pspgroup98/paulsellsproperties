# Privacy Request Process

Internal workflow for handling visitor privacy requests.

**Last updated:** August 2026

---

## Who Can Submit a Request

Any individual who has submitted a contact form, scheduled a Calendly appointment, or
otherwise provided personal information to paulsellsproperties.com.

---

## Types of Requests

| Request type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Know / Access** | What personal information do you hold about me? |
| **Delete** | Delete the personal information you hold about me |
| **Correct** | Update or correct inaccurate personal information |
| **Opt-out (analytics)** | Stop collecting analytics data about me from this device |

---

## How to Submit a Request

Visitors submit requests by:
1. Emailing Paul.Adams@CBRealty.com with subject line "Privacy Request"
2. Calling 301-906-6252 and asking to make a privacy request
3. Sending a message via the contact form at paulsellsproperties.com/contact.html

**Note:** Requests submitted by email will be responded to within a reasonable time,
generally within 30 days.

---

## Internal Handling Procedure

### Step 1 — Receive and log the request

Note the date received, type of request, and contact information provided.

### Step 2 — Identify what data we hold

| Data source | Where to look | Notes |
|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Contact form submissions | Email inbox (search Formspree notification emails from the requestor's email address) | |
| Home valuation form submissions | Email inbox (same search) | |
| Calendly bookings | Calendly dashboard (filter by email) | Calendly manages its own data; may need to direct requestor there |
| GA4 analytics | GA4 does not store identified personal information — analytics data is anonymous and cannot be attributed to a specific individual | No action for analytics |
| Clarity session recordings | Clarity stores session data anonymously — cannot be linked to a specific individual without a Clarity user ID | No action unless requestor provides Clarity session ID |

### Step 3 — Respond to the request

- **Know/Access:** Summarize what form submissions exist in the inbox from that email address and confirm that analytics data cannot be attributed to an identified individual.
- **Delete:** Delete the Formspree notification emails from the inbox. Log the Calendly contact and delete from the Calendly dashboard. Confirm in writing that deletion is complete.
- **Correct:** Update the information in any relevant records.
- **Opt-out (analytics):** Direct the requestor to `privacy.html` → "Manage Analytics Preferences" to opt out on their device.

### Step 4 — Confirm completion

Send a brief email confirming what action was taken and when.

---

## Scope Limitations

- **Analytics data** (GA4, Clarity) is collected without identification — it cannot be linked to a specific person. There is nothing to delete or provide.
- **Calendly data** is managed by Calendly and subject to Calendly's own privacy rights process.
- **Formspree data** at Formspree's servers: direct requestors to [formspree.io/privacy-requests](https://formspree.io) for data they hold beyond what was forwarded to our inbox.

---

## Record-Keeping

Log each request in a simple spreadsheet or note:
- Date received
- Type of request
- How it was handled
- Date completed
