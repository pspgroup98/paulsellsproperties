Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026  ·  Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Paul Adams II ("I," "me," "my") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit paulsellsproperties.com (the "Site"). I am a California-licensed real estate salesperson affiliated with Coldwell Banker Residential Brokerage Company (CA DRE# 00616212).

Plain English summary: I use standard analytics tools to see how many people read articles and which pages are most useful. I do not use advertising pixels, cross-site tracking, or retargeting. I do not sell your personal information. If you use a contact form, your message is transmitted through a third-party form service.

1. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically by the third-party services described below. I do not configure these services to collect your name, email address, or phone number.

Google Analytics 4 Usage analytics

Pages visited, session duration, general location (country/city — not precise), browser type, referring site, scroll depth. IP address is processed by Google and is not stored by me.

policies.google.com/privacy
Microsoft Clarity Session recordings and heatmaps

Mouse movements, clicks, scroll behavior, and pages visited. Clarity's handling of form input content depends on the masking settings configured for this project.

privacy.microsoft.com
Google Fonts Typography

Google's servers deliver the fonts used on this site and may log your IP address. No cookies are set by Google Fonts.

developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy
GitHub Pages Hosting

GitHub collects IP addresses and user-agent strings for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

github.com/site-policy

Advertising pixels: This site does not use Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads, or any other advertising or remarketing pixel. Analytics services are configured without advertising features (Google Signals is disabled; ad personalization is disabled).

2. Scheduling — Calendly

If you click "Start a Conversation" or any scheduling button, this site dynamically loads Calendly software on your device. Calendly operates under its own privacy policy and collects information necessary to complete the scheduling process, including your name, email address, and selected appointment time. This Site does not receive or store Calendly booking data directly — Calendly sends booking confirmations to the inbox associated with my Calendly account.

Calendly assets are not loaded until you click a scheduling button.

3. Information You Provide Through Forms

This Site includes a contact form, a home valuation request form, and a newsletter subscription form. When you submit a form, the information you enter — which may include your name, email address, phone number, and message — is transmitted to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form processing service, and forwarded to my business email address.

Formspree stores submitted data according to its privacy policy. I use form submissions only to respond to your inquiry. I do not add form submissions to marketing lists without your separate consent.

I do not send the contents of form submissions to Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, or any other analytics service. Analytics events record only that a form was started or submitted, not what was written in it.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity set cookies in your browser to distinguish visitors across sessions. These are analytics cookies, not advertising cookies.

This Site stores a single preference in your browser's localStorage under the key psp_analytics. This value records whether you have opted out of analytics and is not transmitted to any server.

5. Your Privacy Choices

You can opt out of analytics at any time. When opted out, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity will not load on any page of this Site.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

You can also opt out of Clarity data collection at privacy.microsoft.com.

6. How Information Is Used

Information collected through analytics is used to understand which pages and articles are most useful to visitors, so I can improve the quality and relevance of the content I publish. I do not use analytics data to make decisions about individual visitors.

Information submitted through contact forms is used solely to respond to your inquiry. I am a licensed California real estate agent; inquiries about property may be shared with parties necessary to complete a transaction (buyers, sellers, lenders, escrow, title), consistent with standard real estate practice.

7. Information Sharing

I do not sell your personal information. I do not share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Information is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With third-party service providers described in this policy (Google, Microsoft, Calendly, Formspree, GitHub) as necessary to operate the Site or respond to your inquiry
  • With parties necessary to fulfill a real estate transaction you have engaged me for
  • If required by law, court order, or regulatory requirement

8. California Residents

California law (CalOPPA) requires websites that collect personal information from California residents to post a privacy policy. This policy satisfies that requirement.

If you are a California resident and would like to know what personal information I hold about you, request that it be deleted, or request a correction, you can contact me using the information below. I will respond within a reasonable time. Note that certain information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations.

This Site does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals, as there is no uniform standard for what such signals require of websites. You may opt out of analytics using the Manage Analytics Preferences button above, which has the same practical effect.

9. Data Retention

Google Analytics data retention is configured in the GA4 property settings. Microsoft Clarity data retention is governed by Microsoft's policy. Form submissions stored by Formspree are subject to Formspree's retention policy. I retain emails containing form submissions in my business email account.

10. Children

This Site is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

This Site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are transmitted via HTTPS to Formspree. No authentication credentials, payment card information, or sensitive personal financial information are collected through this Site.

12. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. I will not retroactively apply material changes to previously collected information in a way that reduces your privacy rights.
