Established Character in the San Fernando Valley

Encino combines large-lot residential properties, a mature commercial corridor, and strong school options in one of the Valley's most established neighborhoods.

Encino is one of the most established residential neighborhoods in the San Fernando Valley, occupying the foothills south of Ventura Boulevard and the valley floor on both sides of Balboa Boulevard. It combines large-lot single-family homes with a mature commercial corridor, strong school options, and the quiet suburban infrastructure that draws long-term Valley residents.

The market is predominantly single-family homes, ranging from ranch-style homes on flat lots to larger hillside estates in the Encino Hills area. The neighborhood has a loyal buyer base drawn from within the Valley and from buyers making a Valley move from the Westside for value and more space.

Encino is an active ADU market. Many properties have ADU potential that adds long-term value for buyers who can use or monetize the additional unit. Factor this into your analysis of single-family purchases here.

What to Know About This Market

Encino has characteristics that affect every transaction. These are the things that matter most for buyers and sellers working here.

For Buyers

  • Encino Hills properties require the standard hillside due diligence: soils, geological assessments, fire zone designation, and insurance availability. Confirm these before entering contract.
  • Encino has several distinct sub-areas with different price levels and buyer profiles. Royal Oaks commands a premium. Know which sub-market your target property is in and what that means for comps and future liquidity.
  • The Ventura Boulevard commercial corridor serves as a functional dividing line in the market. Properties above and below Ventura have different characteristics and price points.
  • Encino is an active ADU market. Many properties have ADU potential that adds value for buyers who can use or monetize the additional unit. Factor this into your analysis.

For Sellers

  • The Encino buyer pool includes move-up Valley buyers, Westside buyers looking for more space at lower prices, and investors. Marketing should address all three audiences.
  • Royal Oaks and Encino Hills properties should be marketed as premium Valley estate properties, not simply Encino. The distinction matters to the target buyer and affects the buyer pool you attract.
  • Updated condition drives premium pricing in Encino. Buyers here are comparing your home to a broad inventory of similar properties. Deferred maintenance is heavily discounted.
  • School district assignment is a genuine driver for family buyers. Know your school assignments and feature them in the marketing where relevant.

Market entry and median price figures are directional estimates based on available market data. Verify current conditions against active MLS data before making any buying or selling decision.

Explore the Neighborhood

What defines Encino as a place to live and why buyers choose it over comparable options elsewhere in Los Angeles.

Royal Oaks

A premium hillside community within Encino with larger lots, mature landscaping, and a strong neighborhood association. The most sought-after addresses in Encino are here, and they command consistent premiums over flat-lot Valley comparables.

Balboa Park and Golf Course

The large public park and 18-hole municipal golf course gives the eastern part of Encino a significant open space amenity. Proximity to the park and lake affects pricing positively for nearby properties.

Ventura Boulevard Corridor

Encino's commercial spine, running east-west through the neighborhood. Restaurants, retail, and services give the neighborhood walkable daily infrastructure without the urban density of Westside alternatives.

Encino Hills

The elevated portion of the neighborhood with canyon views, larger lot sizes, and a more rural character than the valley floor. Access through Mulholland Drive gives this area a sense of separation from the broader Encino market.
