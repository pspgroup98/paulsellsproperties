About West Adams

One of LA's Most Compelling Emerging Markets

West Adams has been one of the more surprising stories in Los Angeles real estate over the past several years. A neighborhood with some of the most intact Victorian and Craftsman housing stock in the city, West Adams fell into decades of disinvestment before a wave of renovation and reinvestment began reshaping it.

The market today is a mix of long-held family homes, recently renovated properties, and investment activity from buyers seeking value and upside. Prices have risen steadily but remain meaningfully below comparable product in Silver Lake, Los Feliz, or the westside.

The Jefferson Park, Harvard Heights, and Adams Normandie sub-areas each have slightly different character and price levels. Buyers willing to research the distinctions will find value that blanket neighborhood searches miss.

What Buyers Should Know

  • West Adams is close to both Culver City and DTLA, with improving transit options. The geographic position is underappreciated by buyers focused on conventional neighborhoods.
  • The neighborhood still carries block-by-block variation in condition and stability. Ground-level research matters more here than online browsing alone.
  • Historical architecture in West Adams can be both a feature and a responsibility. Research renovation costs and any city preservation designations before you buy.
  • Rental demand is strong and growing. ADU potential on many lots adds meaningful investment value.

What Sellers Should Know

  • The West Adams story is compelling to buyers who know it. Make sure your marketing tells it clearly.
  • Authentic architectural preservation resonates more with buyers here than generic renovation. Lead with what makes the property original.
  • Your buyer pool likely includes buyers priced out of Silver Lake and Culver City. Reach them through targeted marketing.
